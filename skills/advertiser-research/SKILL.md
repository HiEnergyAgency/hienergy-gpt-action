# Advertiser Research

Research an advertiser quickly and return a source-backed brief.

## Workflow

1. Capture scope
   - Confirm advertiser name and country/region if ambiguous.
   - Confirm depth: quick snapshot vs detailed brief.

2. Collect source material
   - Use web search for official site, investor/about pages, recent news, and trusted third-party summaries.
   - Prioritize primary sources (official site, filings, investor relations, earnings material).

3. Extract key facts
   - Company overview (what they sell, who they serve)
   - Products/services and pricing model (if visible)
   - Geographic footprint
   - Marketing channels and acquisition hints
   - Affiliate/partner program signals (network listings, partner pages, referral terms)

4. Validate and de-duplicate
   - Cross-check critical facts across at least two credible sources when possible.
   - Mark uncertain claims as unverified.

5. Deliver the brief
   - Use the output template in `references/output-template.md`.
   - Include citations as raw URLs.

## Quality bar

- Prefer recent sources (<18 months) for strategy/channel claims.
- Separate facts from inference.
- Do not invent commission rates, conversion metrics, or spend.
- If data is unavailable, explicitly say "Not publicly disclosed.".
